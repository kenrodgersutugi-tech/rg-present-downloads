RG-Present

Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

RG-Present has no accounts, no tracking and no analytics. Your songs, your services and your settings stay on your computer. We cannot see what your church sings, reads or shows.

What stays on your computer

Everything you put into RG-Present is held on the machine it is installed on, and is never sent anywhere:

  • songs, the service order, themes, media and settings;
  • Bible translations, whether the ones included or ones your church imports;
  • a log file, written so a fault can be explained if you ask for help. It stays on the machine. Nothing sends it to us; if you want us to see it, you send it yourself.

Removing RG-Present leaves this data in place so an upgrade does not lose your library. Deleting the data folder removes it for good.

When RG-Present uses the internet

Everything except the three things below works with no internet at all, and an unreachable server never interrupts a service.

Checking for a new version Nothing of yours. The program asks GitHub what the newest release is, the way any web page asks for a file. In the Microsoft Store version this does not happen at all: the Store does the updating. GitHub
The built-in web browser Whatever you type into it, in the ordinary way of a browser. Cookies and history from it are kept on your computer, apart from the rest of the program, and are not read by RG-Present or sent to us. The sites you visit, and DuckDuckGo if you search

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no usage statistics, no crash reporting.
  • No advertising, and nothing sold or passed to anybody else. Ever.
  • No accounts to create, and no personal details asked for.
  • No access to your camera, microphone or location. The browser refuses those requests.

Children

RG-Present is used in churches, where children are often in the room and sometimes at the desk. It collects nothing from anybody, of any age.

Keeping the licence record

The licence service keeps the licence key, the machine fingerprint and the dates of activation, so that a licence can be used on the number of machines it was bought for. That record is kept while the licence is live and deleted when it ends. Ask us and we will tell you what is held for your licence, or delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version appears on this page.
